I'm a **PhD researcher in nuclear physics at the University of Brighton**. I study the structure of neutron-rich nuclei through β–γ spectroscopy, lifetime measurements and isomeric states.

My PhD research uses data from KISS at RIKEN to study neutron-rich hafnium and tantalum. I also analyse storage-ring data from GSI for mass and half-life measurements. My work brings together experimental spectroscopy, detector systems and analysis with Python, ROOT, Radware and C++.
