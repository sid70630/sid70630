## Hi, I'm Siddharth 👋

I'm a PhD researcher in nuclear physics at the University of Brighton. I study the structure of neutron-rich nuclei through β–γ spectroscopy, lifetime measurements and isomeric states.

My research focuses on hafnium and tantalum isotopes studied at KISS, RIKEN. I also analyse storage-ring data from GSI for mass and half-life measurements. I use Python, ROOT and Radware to turn experimental data into nuclear-structure results.
